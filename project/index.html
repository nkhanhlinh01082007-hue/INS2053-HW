Technology grows when we build together

The Student Tech Club brings curious students together to explore programming, robotics, design, and emerging technology. Whether you are writing your first line of code or developing a new project, you can find teammates, practical guidance, and a welcoming place to learn.

Members collaborate on hands-on projects, exchange ideas, and share what they learn through demonstrations and workshops. Our goal is to make technology approachable and creative while helping every student build confidence and useful skills.
